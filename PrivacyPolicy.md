## Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the **HearChinese** application (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, created by **Marcus Ivan Gerard Vos** (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is provided "AS IS."

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
The Application itself does not require registration and does not directly obtain or store any personally identifiable information from you. 

However, the Application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.
*   **AdMob:** Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and performance data to provide personalized ads and analytics.

### 2. Audio and Voice Data
The Application includes a voice recording feature to assist in learning Chinese tones. 
*   **Local Storage Only:** All audio recordings are processed and stored strictly on your local device's internal storage. 
*   **No Transmission:** Audio data is **never** transmitted to the Service Provider's servers or any third parties. You may delete these recordings at any time by clearing the Application's cache or uninstalling it.

### 3. Location Information
This Application does not collect **precise** real-time location information. However, third-party service providers (AdMob) may use your IP address to determine **approximate location** (such as city or region) to serve relevant advertisements.

### 4. Third-Party Access
Only aggregated, non-personally identifiable data from third-party SDKs (AdMob) is processed to improve the service and manage advertisements. No voice data or personal files are ever shared.

### 5. Data Deletion and Opt-Out
*   **No Account Data:** Because the Application does not require an account or store personal data on external servers, there is no centralized data to delete.
*   **Local Data:** Users can "delete" all local data and voice recordings by uninstalling the Application or clearing storage via system settings.
*   **Ad Identifiers:** You can opt-out of personalized advertising by resetting your Advertising ID in your Android device settings.

### 6. Children
The Application does not knowingly collect data from or market to children under 13. While the `RECORD_AUDIO` permission is used for educational playback, this data is never sent off the device. If you believe a child has provided data to us, please contact imustlanguages@gmail.com

### 7. Security
The Service Provider is committed to safeguarding your privacy. Since the Application does not collect personal data on external servers, the risk of a traditional data breach is minimized.

### 8. Changes and Consent
This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Your continued use of the Application signifies your approval of the current policy.
